# Mosaic Singularity — Federal Contract Readiness Checklist
_Canadian federal procurement · prepared 2 November 2026_

Tick each box. Every link below was verified live on 2 Nov 2026.

---

## Phase 0 — Orientation (≈1 hour)

- [ ] Read the 5 official pages in order: https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/acquisitions/selling-federal-government.html
- [ ] Skim the PAC support hub: https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/acquisitions/support-for-businesses.html
- [ ] Register for one free seminar/event: https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/programs/pac/event-calendar.html

## Phase 1 — Foundation (before registering)

- [ ] CRA business number confirmed (it is on HST/payroll correspondence): https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/registering-your-business/register.html
- [ ] Legal name + head-office civic address match CRA exactly (verify via GST/HST Registry Search): https://www.businessregistration-inscriptionentreprise.gc.ca/ebci/brom/registry/pub/reg_01_Ld.action
- [ ] Shared inbox chosen to own the government account (e.g. `contracts@mosaicsingularity.com` — NOT a personal address)
- [ ] Named main administrator + backup person, with access to payment/banking information
- [ ] Business documents folder created: articles of incorporation, BN confirmation, banking info, insurance certificates, 3 case studies, CVs, rate card

## Phase 2 — Register on CanadaBuys (≈2 hours)

- [ ] Open the registration checklist and have it beside you: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/support/use-checklist-prepare-register-sap-business-network
- [ ] Register via the CanadaBuys route (this attaches PSPC as a customer): https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/getting-started/preparing-sell-government/how-register-your-business
- [ ] Follow the detailed guide if stuck: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/support/registering-sap-ariba-guide-businesses
- [ ] Activation email clicked, account live: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/account-access
- [ ] **ANID# recorded** (profile menu, top-right → Company Profile) ______________________
- [ ] Questionnaire Q3–Q8 completed (Code of Conduct consent, industry, business type, owners/directors incl. any 5%+ holders, Indigenous/diversity self-ID optional, environment)
- [ ] Q9 **full registration** completed (CRA BN + financial info) — removes delay at award
- [ ] Q12 skipped unless pursuing DND/defence work
- [ ] Confirmed only ONE SAP Business Network account exists under our business number

## Phase 3 — Opportunity radar (weekly habit, Mondays)

- [ ] 5 saved searches created with email/web-feed alerts: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities
- [ ] Search technique reviewed: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/how-to-find-tender-opportunities
- [ ] Understood what government actually buys in our categories: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/what-government-buys
- [ ] Contract history pulled for 10 comparable awards (who won, value, department): https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/support/searching-award-notices-and-contract-history
- [ ] Know how to read notice types: RFP · RFQ/ITT · RFI · ACAN · SO/SA calls

## Phase 4 — Pre-qualification (the real lever)

- [ ] Standing offers / supply arrangements understood and monitored: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/standing-offers-and-supply-arrangements
- [ ] Registered in professional-services sourcing tools (ProServices etc.) if we sell expertise: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/support/register-our-sourcing-tools-professional-services
- [ ] Security clearance need assessed; Contract Security Program started if any target opportunity flags it: https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/industrial-security/security-requirements-contracting.html
- [ ] Indigenous Business Directory reviewed — apply only if genuinely 51%+ Indigenous-owned/controlled: https://services.sac-isc.gc.ca/REA-IBD?lang=eng
- [ ] Innovative Solutions Canada reviewed if we have a market-ready innovation: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovative-solutions-canada/en
- [ ] Buy Canadian Policy read for positioning language: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/buy-canadian-policy

## Phase 5 — Bidding

- [ ] Code of Conduct + supplier integrity read (mandatory attestation in every bid): https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/standards-oversight/supplier-integrity-compliance.html
- [ ] Bid playbook built: compliance matrix, mandatory-criteria checklist, page-limit rules, submission 48h early
- [ ] Bidding guidance reviewed: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/how-bid-tender-opportunities
- [ ] PAC bidding page reviewed: https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/acquisitions/support-for-businesses/bid-on-opportunities.html
- [ ] Ombud contact known for fairness concerns: https://opo-boa.gc.ca/index-eng.html

## Phase 6 — Award & delivery

- [ ] Full registration validated: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/support/verifying-supplier-full-registration-sap-ariba
- [ ] "Email orders to" set to the monitored shared inbox
- [ ] Insurance confirmed (CGL; professional liability for services)
- [ ] Invoicing matches the PO in SAP Business Network; keep proof of delivery
- [ ] Debrief requested after every unsuccessful bid

### Watch-outs
- Registration is **free**. Do not pay a third party for "government supplier registration".
- The old **SRI / Procurement Business Number** route is retired — do not follow guides that tell you to obtain a PBN.
- One SAP Business Network account per CRA business number. Duplicates get deactivated.
- Mandatory criteria are pass/fail. A brilliant bid that misses one line scores zero.
